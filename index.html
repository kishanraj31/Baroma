About Baroma

Our Story

At Baroma, we are passionate about bringing the rich flavors of Bihari cuisine to life. Our chefs use traditional recipes passed down through generations, combined with fresh, locally sourced ingredients to create authentic dishes that celebrate the culinary heritage of Bihar.

Founded in 2030, Baroma has quickly become a beloved destination for food enthusiasts seeking genuine Bihari flavors. Our commitment to quality and authenticity has earned us a loyal following, and we take pride in sharing our love for Bihari cuisine with every guest who walks through our doors.
